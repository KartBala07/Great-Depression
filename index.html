How It Works

  • You are someone the world has forgotten — with one idea it can't afford to.
  • Each season, you take a single action: chase the work, build, sell, scrape by, or rest.
  • Four things keep your story alive: Money, Heart, Name, and The Work.
  • People drift in and out — someone you love, someone who wants you to fail. What you choose changes how it ends.
  • Finish the work and you make history. Run out of money or heart, and the story ends another way.
