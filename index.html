You have a workbench, a head full of ideas, and the worst economy in living memory. Build something the world can't afford to ignore.

A historical strategy game. Every year brings a real event from the Great Depression.

How to Play

  • You are an inventor in 1929, the month the market crashed. Survive the decade and leave a legacy.
  • Each turn is a season. You take one action per season: research, build a prototype, sell, take odd jobs, or rest.
  • Watch four meters: Cash keeps you alive, Morale keeps you going, Reputation opens doors, and Progress finishes your invention.
  • Events strike — dust storms, bank runs, a rival, a hungry neighbor. Your choices shape your story.
  • If Cash or Morale hits zero, your dream ends. Finish your invention to win.
